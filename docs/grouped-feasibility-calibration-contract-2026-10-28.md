# Fixed feasibility calibration development experiment

Motivation: frozen feasibility scores made false-positive hardware judgments on tiny-regex; the Sudoku agent spent three probes on infeasible designs, while nanocobs tied merit. Assess one calibration layer on the existing model, not a model-family or hyperparameter sweep.

Use the authenticated expanded consumed-development corpus: 156 successful candidate rows across 17 source groups, four charged historical failures. Recent workloads are consumed development data now. For every outer held-out source group remove all its canonical candidate families from training and inner calibration. For each remaining inner workload, remove its group and families before fitting the unchanged 20-member XGBoost ensemble. Fit a logistic calibration of clipped-logit inner out-of-fold scores at +64 LUT4/12MHz using equal total weight per workload; C=1, seed0, five-group minimum. Use unchanged scores when inner labels are one-class, too few groups remain, or the fitted slope is nonpositive. No hyperparameter selection based on outer outcomes.

Primary endpoint: source-macro best verified feasible cycle reduction at budget3; also budgets1/2, regret where oracle complete, charged failures and measured evaluation time. Compare calibrated factorized utility against unchanged raw utility, pairwise XGBoost, merit, calibrated latency, frequency and five seeded random policies. Cap effective budget by actual pool size. Five random seeds are dependent within a source, not independent experiments. Report Brier/log-loss secondarily; improved probabilities alone do not justify promotion. This is a fixed feasibility-component comparison, not a complete model-selection study; the existing pairwise ranker remains the deployed challenger.

Inner calibration is fit only on successful known outcomes; failures remain charged unknowns during outer selection replay. No target labels enter ordering. Save outer/inner identity lists and predictions, protocol/source hashes, costs, every result and negative finding. A logistic calibration fit does not guarantee calibration on a new workload, and ensemble spread remains uncalibrated. No automatic production promotion. A candidate change would require a separately frozen forward test; countnegative candidate outcomes remain unmeasured during this development experiment.
