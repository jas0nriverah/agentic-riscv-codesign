# Specialization after V13: preserve the control outside one structural domain

Research/design note, 2026-10-16. No gate membership counts, routed scores, fits, features, solver queries or native measurements were computed for this note. The parent owns the single gate recipe and its release before scoring.

V13 provides a reason to test specialization, not to deploy its global update. New high-product-family MAE falls from **1396.285 to 36.613 LUT4**, while original 1,713 macro MAE rises **5.16%**; original-cohort cap 64 false rejections and maximum underprediction also worsen. [Actual matched results](v13-coverage-results.md) support asking whether a narrowly routed use of the coverage-trained model can preserve useful coverage without changing unrelated predictions. They do not establish that routing succeeds. V9 remains retained.

## Five primary sources and what transfers

1. **MasterRTL, expanded TCAD paper (2025).** Its area predictor combines SOG-derived information with a lightweight tree model. Transfer models learn differences across synthesis/layout stages or technology libraries from existing predictions and design-scale/library information. Evaluation uses 90 RTL designs and tenfold cross-validation; its 45-design augmentation study examines TNS transfer and path slack. The paper reports improvements within those tasks, rather than a guarantee that augmentation helps every target. This supports representative data and explicit transfer conditions; ASIC area/timing results are not isolated iCE40 LUT4 accuracy or backward-compatibility evidence. [Author-hosted paper, II-D–F and III-A/D/E](https://zhiyaoxie.com/files/TCAD25_MasterRTL.pdf).

2. **StructRTL, “Beyond Tokens” (arXiv v1, 2025).** Structural masked-node/edge pretraining supplies CDFG representations; an optional post-mapping teacher supervises the earlier-stage predictor. The dataset contains 13,200 designs, split 80/20; labels come from Yosys/ABC mapping with SkyWater 130 nm. Table 1 reports area R² 0.7463 without distillation. The paper applies logarithmic target transforms and emphasizes relative design quality, so its reported metrics cannot be read as our raw-LUT4 cap errors. This is a representation/distillation method, not evidence for our particular gate or a regression-free update. [Primary paper, quality estimation, distillation, experiments and appendix](https://arxiv.org/html/2508.18730v1).

3. **LEAPER (ICCD 2022).** A source FPGA performance/resource model uses random-forest and gradient-boosting learners; target adaptation selects between TrAdaBoost and Gaussian-process transfer learners. Evaluation units are accelerator configurations across six HLS workloads and five target FPGAs, including BRAM/FF/LUT/DSP usage. Its abstract reports approximately 85% accuracy with 5-shot transfer, and Section 3.4 defines relative-error evaluation. Algorithm 1 also uses error on the configurations outside its transfer subset for learner selection. Our inference: any reproduction must account for selection labels and separate the final test; the few-shot headline is not a free label budget or an isolated-circuit guarantee. [Primary paper, Sections 2.4–2.6, 3.4 and 4](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2208.10606), [author institution publication](https://research.ibm.com/publications/leaper-fast-and-accurate-fpga-based-system-performance-prediction-via-transfer-learning).

4. **Adaptive Mixtures of Local Experts (1991).** A gating network allocates inputs to experts and localizes learning; its demonstrated task is vowel discrimination. It establishes specialization as longstanding methodology, not a new ISAForge invention. Our fixed semantic-pattern gate is simpler than its jointly learned probabilistic router, and the paper offers no PPA-transfer or safety guarantee. [Author-hosted original paper](https://www.cs.toronto.edu/~fritz/absps/jjnh91.pdf).

5. **Positive-Congruent Training (CVPR 2021).** Focal distillation weights compatibility with an old classifier, particularly on formerly correct cases. ImageNet/iNaturalist experiments distinguish overall error from negative flips. In Table 2a, one focal-distillation setting lowers negative flips from 6.44% to 2.35% while classification error changes from 30.29% to 30.47%: compatibility and accuracy are separate endpoints. This motivates tracking newly introduced cap-decision errors, not importing the classifier loss or calling a continuous area regressor safe. [Official proceedings](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/CVPR2021/html/Yan_Positive-Congruent_Training_Towards_Regression-Free_Model_Updates_CVPR_2021_paper.html), [paper/Table 2](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2011.09161).

## One gate, fixed before its routed scores

Use the parent's single definition in [evaluate_v14_specialist.py](../scripts/evaluate_v14_specialist.py): an original formula contains `mulh`, `mulhu` or `mulhsu`, and either operand's subtree contains an exact frozen V7 `or_sub` or `sub_and` correlation whose shared atom syntactically contains an input.

The identities are `(z | K) − z = (~z) & K` and `z − (z & K) = z & ~K`, in wrapping 32-bit arithmetic. Match the existing routine; do not add normalization, sign/width/support cutoffs, learned thresholds, literal-mask lists, candidate IDs or family-name routing. This includes correlations nested below a multiplier operand and either signedness/operand position. It does **not** prove restricted width, nonnegativity or semantic nonconstancy: masks may leave full support, and an input-containing atom can cancel to a constant. Original-RTL targets and the original-generic-zero override remain unchanged.

For row `i` and paired seed `s`, define:

`routed(i,s) = augmented_OOF(i,s) if gate(formula_i) else control_OOF(i,s)`.

This reuses the coverage-trained model as an expert; it does not train a new specialist. Outside the gate, exact equality to the matched control is guaranteed by construction. Inside it, even mean error or cap safety is unproved. The control here is the V13 fold-matched control, **not** the published full-data V9 artifact.

## Fair next experiment and release boundary

1. Freeze exactly one gate, source versions, input bindings and endpoints before constructing routed forecasts. Use all six saved V13 OOF point files with their common 538 closed groups, five folds and three seeds. Both existing points already exclude the entire validation group from training, including linked new rows. No additional fits, gate search or favorable-case replacement.
2. Preserve all 1,761 formulas. Report routed rows **and distinct closed groups** by cohort, stratum and multiplier kind, including zero counts. Score old 1,713 equal-nine-stratum macro MAE as primary; new 48 family/stratum errors remain secondary. Show control, global augmentation and routed results for every seed, plus exact unchanged nongated predictions. If no old row routes, unchanged old scores are a mechanical property, not evidence of old-domain repair.
3. Keep cap 64 false accepts and false rejects separate, with full denominators and newly introduced/fixed threshold-decision errors. Retain cap 32/128, P99, worst absolute error and maximum underprediction. Aggregate compatibility cannot certify every routed formula. Do not select a seed or claim population confidence from three algorithm seeds.
4. Call this **exploratory consumed-development analysis**. The gate family was motivated by observed V13 outcomes; freezing it before its own scoring does not turn those outcomes into fresh validation. A good result can justify a separate final-artifact release, not automatic promotion.
5. Before fresh labels, freeze the final base/expert artifacts, gate, feature/proof code, target toolchain, selection policy and total budget. Reserve complete eligible pools from genuinely unconsumed source groups, with accepted aliases and feature-collision closure kept away from fitting data. Report source-weighted full-pool errors and gate-positive/negative denominators, including empty pools. No source swaps because a gate is rare or a result disappoints. If there are no eligible routed cases, expert transfer is unassessed. Charge any calibration/adaptation labels separately and never recycle the fresh test for gate revision.

The plausible contribution is an auditable, mechanism-specific update policy for this measured circuit task, with paired error and decision-compatibility evidence. Graph representation, data augmentation, transfer and expert routing already have substantial prior art; ISAForge itself already tried a multiply-based router in V6. This narrower correlation gate, existing V7 identities and V13 coverage data need a demonstrated benefit under the above comparison before any claim of methodological novelty or practical improvement.

The subsequent [V14 development result](v14-specialist-results.md) preserves the matched original control and improves the new coverage mean, while retaining worse new-cohort underprediction. It does not satisfy the fresh-transfer requirement above.
