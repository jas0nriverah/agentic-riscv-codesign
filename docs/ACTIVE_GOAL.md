# Active ISAForge research goal

The owner requested this goal on October 25, 2026: continue improving ISAForge until measured evidence supports superiority over strong established methods. After a requested stopping point, the owner explicitly resumed development on October 25. The goal is active and remains unachieved. No superiority or completion is established.

Success means better verified feasible whole-program performance under matched evaluation budgets on a declared untouched workload cohort, compared with the strongest heuristics and appropriate published-method implementations. It is limited to the supported architecture/workload contract; it is not a claim of beating every ASIP system or published peak speedup.

- Preserve the original proposal, existing measurements, failures and frozen sources.
- First implement/test joint covariance and a consistent paid-update procedure. Compare one-step and finite two-step decisions with identical models, utility and information. Keep all fourteen existing controls and a simple final-query heuristic fallback.
- Use consumed-data development to diagnose changes. Freeze policies, models, source/family exclusions, inputs, budgets and measurement controllers before fresh candidate outcomes. Qualifying an original workload is distinct from measuring candidate labels.
- A published-method comparison must identify the reproduced algorithm, surrogate/hyperparameter procedure, information and costs. A deliberately weak fixed GP plus an acquisition is an adaptation/control, not proof of superiority over the published system.
- Seek a confirmatory cohort of at least six eligible independent source families, with prespecified +64-LUT4 /12-MHz /three-query primary outcome and one/two-query curves. Record every qualification rejection. Repeated seeds, contexts and candidates do not increase the independent-source count. Report paired source-level uncertainty; an imprecise or negative result leaves the goal unachieved.
- Report best verified feasible cycle reduction, regret to a complete measured-pool oracle where available, every comparator win/loss, hardware resources/timing/correctness, target evaluation count/time, historical training measurements and host computation. No automatic stopping or total-cost win may be inferred from early oracle attainment alone.
- Complete jointly measured compatible instruction sets after single-instruction selection works. Keep RTL rewriting, unrelated model/framework work, cloud and demo development outside scope. The later LLM comparison must hold predictors, public information and measurement budgets fixed.
