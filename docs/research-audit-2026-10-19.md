# ISAForge research audit — October 19, 2026

ISAForge has a useful experimental foundation, a real development improvement, and several informative failures. Its strongest next research question is whether a small, structure-aware predictor can reduce hardware-evaluation cost while preserving good processor choices on new applications. That has not yet been demonstrated. Chasing a lower percentage error on repeatedly inspected circuits would not establish it.

This audit inspected the repository at `dc9ae1654def623954abfdec1768b44b3ec0eab3`, its retained results and current model/measurement code, and primary literature across RTL prediction, transfer learning, custom-instruction design, decision-focused learning, and uncertainty. No training, inference, synthesis, new labels, or automation restart was performed. Recommendations below are proposed experiments, not achieved improvements. The existing [handoff](HANDOFF.md) remains authoritative about incomplete execution stages.

**2. The principal weaknesses are separable**

Large arithmetic errors and infeasible selections need different remedies. In V9's historical risk diagnosis, multiplication contributes 50.30% of total absolute error and arithmetic chains 25.34%. However, none of the six cap64 false accepts are multiplications: three are comparators, and the others are a parallel branch, a constant shift, and a variable shift. A multiplication-focused model can improve headline MAE while leaving every dangerous cap64 decision unchanged. [Risk diagnosis](v9-coverage-results.md).

Our features already include considerable structural and semantic information. V7 tested proved rewrites and bit/affine descriptors; its best development view did not produce a primary win in the subsequent V8 application comparison. Simply adding signedness, known bits, or more graph columns is not a new remedy. The useful question is which missing mechanism causes an error, whether its representation distinguishes the relevant cases, and whether training contains independent examples of it. [V7](v7-development-results.md), [V8](v8-application-results.md), [coverage diagnosis](v12-model-direction.md).

Acquisition also matters. V9's local residual adapter improved MAE only 1.94% with three charged reveals per family. All eight video formulas containing low multiplication had zero tree-leaf proximity to queried support, so their predictions could not change. This was a failure of query coverage as well as model adaptation. A new kernel alone is not enough if the query schedule never reaches the relevant structures. [Adaptation result](v9-adaptation-results.md).

The uncertainty story is incomplete. V9 has no released calibrated upper margin, and V6's old margin does not transfer automatically. The largest V8 miss was inside every training feature's min/max range, so per-feature range checks cannot detect all structural novelty. Report disagreement and distance as diagnostics until their relationship to actual failure is validated. [V8](v8-application-results.md), [hardware tradeoffs](hardware-tradeoffs.md), [model implementation](../isaforge/area_model_v6.py).

**3. What the wider literature changes**

| Primary source | Relevant evidence or method | Implication for ISAForge |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [MasterRTL, ICCAD 2023](https://arxiv.org/abs/2311.08441) | Bit-level simple-operator graphs and separate PPA estimation models; evaluation on 90 RTL designs | Structural representation with a small learner is a serious baseline. Our iCE40 adaptation is not a reproduction of the paper's ASIC results. Correlation is not percentage accuracy. |
| [StructRTL, inspected 2025 v1](https://arxiv.org/html/2508.18730v1) | CDFG pretraining and optional post-mapping teacher; 13,200 designs, 80/20 split, SkyWater130 labels. Area R² rises 0.7463 → 0.8676 with distillation, but reported area MAE worsens 0.3649 → 0.3856. Targets are log-transformed. | Representation/distillation merits a bounded ablation, not an assumption that all metrics improve. Verify metric scale before comparing reported MAPE with our raw LUT4 errors. These numbers refer to the preprint version, not a checked final proceedings table. |
| [UniRTL, ICML 2026](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v306/liu26fw.html) | Joint code/CDFG representations, mutual masked modeling and staged alignment | Current research goes beyond operator counts. Start with a small structural encoder; a full multimodal foundation model is not justified by our present evidence or budget. |
| [LEAPER, ICCD 2022](https://arxiv.org/abs/2208.10606) | Abstract reports average 85% accuracy with five-shot transfer across six workloads/five FPGAs and 10× exploration-time reduction | Compare explicit target-label budgets and end-to-end cost. Its HLS accelerator configurations and accuracy definition are not our isolated-circuit test. Count learner-selection labels as well as adaptation labels. |
| [CIDRE, 2025](https://arxiv.org/html/2509.15782v1) | Integrates custom instructions into a processor model and considers clock-period changes. Reports up to 2.47× estimated speedup, below 24% area increase, with eight instructions and 32/28nm ASIC synthesis | This is a closer systems comparison than a PPA regressor alone. Its CPI=1 runtime model, processor, I/O choices, and technology differ from ours; compare methods under matched conditions. |
| [Smart “Predict, then Optimize”](https://arxiv.org/abs/1710.08005) | Trains predictions around downstream decision loss | Evaluate selection regret and feasibility, not only regression error. Its particular optimization assumptions are not automatically satisfied by our area-constrained selector. |
| [Conformal Risk Control](https://arxiv.org/abs/2208.02814) and [weighted risk control under covariate shift](https://arxiv.org/abs/2501.11413) | Calibration connects predictions with explicit risk objectives under stated assumptions | Use disjoint calibration groups and measure risk versus abstention. These methods do not promise safety under arbitrary new arithmetic mechanisms or technology changes. |
| [qNEHVI, NeurIPS 2021](https://proceedings.neurips.cc/paper_files/paper/2021/hash/11704817e347269b7254e744b5e22dac-Abstract.html) | Sample-efficient multiobjective search with uncertain Pareto fronts | A later comparison can allocate expensive routing jobs across area/runtime tradeoffs. It needs credible uncertainty and a cheap heuristic baseline first. |

Two additional directions are useful but secondary: [DeepCircuitX](https://arxiv.org/abs/2502.18297) supplies repository-level RTL/netlist/PPA material for representation experiments; [RTL-OPT](https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.01765) supplies 36 paired suboptimal/human-optimized designs and correctness/PPA evaluation. Neither provides automatically compatible iCE40 labels. Check licenses and source-family overlap before importing data. Optimization-equivalent RTL may map differently: keep representation rewrites separate from physically implemented rewrites and measure the actual implementation being predicted.

**4. Priority order for improvement**

| Priority | Work | Required evidence before claiming success |
| --- | --- | --- |
| P0 | Finish the already prepared V11 frozen V6/V9 comparison | All 327 paired predictions frozen before labels; complete original-RTL target collection and audit; unchanged primary endpoint and every failure retained. |
| P1 | Test one prospective structural specialist against the retained base | New gate-positive structures and new applications; lower within-gate error, separately reported underprediction/false accepts, exact fallback outside the gate. |
| P1 | Compare acquisition strategies at equal label budget | Targeted, random, and structural-diversity acquisition under identical learners/splits; compare both prediction and cap decisions. Add uncertainty/boundary acquisition only as a predefined arm. |
| P1 | Build a useful decision benchmark | At fixed synthesis budgets, compare merit-only, random, model-guided and fully measured oracle choices; measure integrated processor outcomes for selected candidates. |
| P2 | Test a small structural representation or distillation ablation | Same training labels/groups, tuning budget and held-out sources as strong tree baselines; cold preprocessing cost included. |
| P2 | Extend to uncertainty-aware area/runtime Pareto search | Improvement in feasible utility or frontier quality per measured target, with routing-seed variation and no hidden label access. |

For P0, the immediate blocker is engineering. All 327 feature extractions and the 806-channel raw replay completed. Packaging exceeded its deadline; fresh paired inference and target measurements have not run. The active feature archiver invokes a whole-tree `rglob` guard for every file/chunk, and the target collector uses the same pattern. This creates roughly quadratic file-inventory work. Repair this with an immutable initial inventory, incremental byte accounting, bounded deadline/free-space checks, and a final full audit. Preserve the failed attempt and create a separate recovery receipt; do not edit its completion to success. [Handoff](HANDOFF.md), [feature runner](../scripts/predict_v11d_transfer.py), [target collector](../scripts/measure_v11_transfer.py).

V11 must remain the existing V6/V9 experiment. Do not slip a newly chosen expert or acquisition rule into its primary comparison. Its five-family primary gives the one-circuit factorial family 20% weight; report the planned leave-family-out sensitivity. Recursion's empty outcome is unavailable, not zero error. Identity-unmatched circuits are not proof of semantic or source independence. Once results are inspected, subsequent revisions need another unconsumed evaluation. [Reporting plan](v11-comparison-reporting-plan.md).

For specialization, the V14 gate is a hypothesis, not a product. It routed 16 new examples and none of the original 1,713; thus unchanged old scores were guaranteed by routing. Its new examples cover two root-`mulhsu` contexts, while the gate admits broader operations and nesting. Freeze a deployable base/expert pair and test that extrapolation explicitly. A later residual expert is another candidate, not a demonstrated improvement. Already tried multiplication routers and V9 residual adaptation remain mandatory controls.

For data acquisition, use a shared input pool and charge every new physical target. Keep a common, independently reserved test pool so that changing queried examples does not change the reported test denominator. Start with a small fixed budget, such as 48 labels per arm, with reuse of identical physical measurements accounted for separately from simulated per-policy charges. Report diversity by independent structure/source groups, not merely different constants. This would isolate the value of targeting; V9's improvement alone cannot distinguish a smart acquisition policy from simply receiving 48 more examples.

For calibration, distinguish a point estimate from a decision. A proposed rule is to accept cheaply only when a validated upper bound is below the area cap, reject confidently infeasible cases, and spend limited measurement budget on ambiguous high-merit candidates. Report false acceptance among accepted choices, acceptance coverage, false rejections, abstentions and label cost. With only a few related families, empirical coverage is descriptive; do not imply a universal guarantee. Calibration and threshold selection require separate groups from the final test.

For learned representations, test a small GNN before a large pretrained text model. Compare it with the retained forest and a bounded, equally tuned boosting baseline. A post-mapping teacher may supervise training designs, but no held-out mapped netlists or targets may enter training or distillation. Include zero-area cases. Do not automatically copy a log target: V12 already falsified that simple change for our primary objective.

**5. Make hardware decisions the research endpoint**

The current area model is not a complete PPA model. Runtime depends on instruction latency, interface and register overhead, stalls, compiler use, overlap between candidates, and processor frequency. For integration, measure actual workload cycles and report both common-clock runtime and runtime at each routed frequency. Keep LUTs, carry cells, FFs, BRAM and packed logic usage separate. Power and energy need their own measurements or explicitly validated models. [Hardware evidence](hardware-tradeoffs.md).

Our existing CRC32 result already shows why this matters: common-clock runtime improves 8.60%, whereas the routed-frequency results differ across seeds and LUT×runtime worsens in two of three pairs. The CSD study similarly chooses the rewritten implementation for only 19 of 32 circuits under its original-resource/clock constraints, despite broad LUT reductions. These are useful tradeoffs to explain, not inconvenient outcomes to average away.

Use naturally varied application pools, instruction-count and resource budgets, and realistic I/O/latency constraints. Freeze the design before evaluating ML. Do not deliberately choose only settings where merit-only loses; a continued tie means ML has not earned its added cost. Small-pool exact optimization can supply an oracle; distinguish an exact oracle from an approximate solver when scaling up.

Charge the whole pipeline: feature extraction, proof/rewriting, inference, synthesis, routing, and orchestration. Cached prediction latency is not deployment latency. For tiny circuits, preprocessing may consume much of the synthesis time being saved; this is a hypothesis to time, not an established speedup. A two-stage policy—cheap structural screening followed by expensive features or synthesis for promising/uncertain cases—is worth testing only if measured total cost improves.

**6. Evaluation and presentation changes**

Keep one declared primary objective and a small fixed secondary set. For prediction, report family-macro MAE, pooled MAE, positive-only MAPE with its denominator, zero-case error, signed bias, P95/P99 error and worst underprediction. For decisions, show cap-specific confusion counts/denominators, newly introduced versus fixed errors, feasible regret and query cost. MAPE is `100 × mean(abs(predicted−measured)/measured)` over positive measured LUT counts; it is not classification accuracy and does not account for zeros. A lower MAPE can coexist with worse budget safety, as V12 demonstrates.

Separate training, model/gate selection, calibration, and final evaluation at source/identity-component level. Repeated estimator seeds measure algorithm variability, not confidence over new applications. With only four or five application families, show each family and leave-one-family-out sensitivity instead of a persuasive-looking population confidence band. Freeze a limited experiment menu and keep negative results; repeatedly inspecting a test makes it development data.

The most useful next figures are: paired V11 per-family errors; error versus true area with cap mistakes marked; old/new and gated/ungated specialist results; feasible regret versus synthesis calls and total wall time; and integrated area/runtime Pareto plots with routing-seed variation. Export source data, PDF/SVG and PNG from saved outcomes. Never put ASIC area, FPGA LUT4, correlation and differently scaled percentage errors on one comparative bar chart.

The project can aim for this claim: **under a fixed evaluation budget, structure-aware prediction and selective measurement find better feasible RISC-V custom-instruction implementations on held-out applications, with measured processor tradeoffs.** Each part still needs evidence. The immediate return on effort is completing the frozen comparison and testing decision value; another large model or broad hyperparameter sweep is lower priority.
