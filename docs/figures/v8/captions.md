# V8 application-transfer figure captions

**Figure 1 — Frozen point-prediction error and family sensitivity.** Panel A reports all six models using equal program weights within a family and equal weights across the four primary source families. The declared primary contrast is legacy rewritten versus mapping V6: MAE 3.431883 versus 3.409191 LUT4, a rewritten-minus-baseline difference of +0.022693 LUT4. Panel B retains every paired family difference; negative differences favor rewritten. Panel C recomputes that descriptive contrast after omitting each family, exposing dependence on the family mix. No population confidence interval is estimated from these four families. Other-model comparisons and omission sensitivity are secondary.

**Figure 2 — Selection regret under a fixed measurement budget.** Panel A uses the declared cap of 64 LUT4 and one candidate attempt per program and policy. The six model policies prioritize predicted-feasible candidates by overlap-safe saved instructions; actual measured feasibility determines the final selection. Rewritten regret is 0.000000% of trace instructions versus 0.605545% for mapping V6. Merit-only regret is 0.000000%; this tie or difference must be retained when assessing model value. Panel B reports the same policies at budgets of one, three and five attempts; all five fixed random seeds are averaged without treating seeds as population replicates. Regret is the full-pool oracle overlap-safe saved-instruction count minus the selected count, divided by the trace instruction count and multiplied by 100. It is an instruction-count proxy, not processor speedup, latency, clock frequency, or additive multi-instruction benefit.

**Figure 3 — Actual/predicted resource counts and consumed-test context.** Panels A–B show mapping V6 and legacy rewritten on primary-family memberships with identical, zero-origin, equal-aspect axes; the dashed line is exact prediction. Panel C shows the eight primary memberships with the largest rewritten absolute errors and the baseline errors on those same cases. Case labels give the program and first six hexadecimal characters of the formula SHA-256; full formulas and range diagnostics accompany the figures in figure-data.json. Panel D distinguishes all 152 program memberships, 146 unique formulas and 150 primary memberships. Input-only novelty classes overlap and do not exclude any primary row; the strict-no-declared-match subset covers only three of four primary families and cannot support a complete four-family novelty claim. Worst-error and novelty panels are secondary diagnostics after test consumption.

All figures preserve the declared primary comparison, show every comparison model, and use zero-inclusive axes. Source/input and output hashes are recorded in figure-manifest.json.
