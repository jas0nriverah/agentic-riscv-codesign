dual_semantics with equally tuned original SOG14. The complete primary scores are
21.68984375 versus 52.90986979 LUT4, a 59.006% reduction. V5 minus SOG is

It is not an application, acquisition-budget, CPU-speedup or energy result.

Keep the full eight-stratum primary; these decompositions explain its mechanism,
not a reason to remove multipliers or replace the endpoint.

Uncertainty is correctly calculated but does not transfer reliably

There are 1,665 fitting formulas in 576 groups and 392 calibration formulas in
145 groups. Independent calibration replay confirms rank 132 at alpha=0.1.
The one-sided margins are 21.15666667 (SOG), 12.34 (original graph), 13.58666667
(dual graph), and 13.58 LUT4 (full semantics). No quantile implementation error
explains the coverage loss.

The full semantics 90% nominal bound covers only 105/128 novel formulas (82.03%)
and 12/19 complete novel clusters (63.16%). Known-family coverage is 125/128
formulas (97.66%) and 91/94 clusters (96.81%). All bounds are finite. Novel
arithmetic coverage is 3/16 formulas and 0/1 cluster; shared diamonds are 10/16
formulas and 2/5 clusters. Variable shifts have 14/16 formula coverage but
0/1 complete-cluster coverage. Formula coverage cannot substitute for the
whole-cluster target. Original graph has still worse whole-cluster coverage
(10/19) despite its best novel point MAE. Even dual graph covers only 14/19.

This is a distribution-shift failure of an exchangeability-dependent bound,
not a demonstrated safe area-cap screen. Raising the margin on these test
outcomes would consume the holdout for development and require a new test.
The calibration strata are also uneven: only two mask-logic rows and 14
multiplier rows, versus 139 historical workload rows; group allocation explains
those counts but cannot establish external family exchangeability.

Limited family replication and bootstrap conditioning

Known-family test inputs contain 94 clusters; novel inputs contain only 19.
Five novel strata each contain one 16-row cluster. The remaining strata have
four multiplier, five parallel and five shared-diamond clusters. Therefore
the five single-family stratum contributions cannot vary between families in
the retained bootstrap draws. Novel intervals use 10,000 valid draws out of
116,355 attempts: 106,355 draws, 91.41%, are rejected for a missing stratum.
Known intervals reject 181 of 10,181 attempts. This declared conditioning is
implemented faithfully, but weakens any interpretation as general population
uncertainty. The primary descriptive interval must be accompanied by these
limits rather than presented as universal confidence or adjusted significance.

Priority next diagnostics and changes, for a new model lineage

1. Validate original graph as a candidate baseline on multiple independently
   reserved families/programs. It is the strongest observed point fit here,
   but non-multiplier gain is small and broad transfer remains unestablished.
   Repeat development training seeds; all current differences compare one
   fixed seed. Adding features changes random tree splits even when the seed
   and estimator recipe are identical, so a poorer full fit does not alone
   prove that the semantic descriptors are intrinsically harmful.

2. Diagnose serial arithmetic and carry mapping. All 16 novel arithmetic rows
   are underpredicted by original graph, dual graph and full semantics. Their
   signed biases are -14.65354, -13.31604 and -19.09958 LUT4, versus SOG's -5.65.
   Use original RTL and retained mapped carry/LUT counts as development analysis
   to test carry-chain/output-sharing hypotheses; do not feed mapped counts
   into an allegedly cheap inference feature. Add deeper, varied arithmetic
   families to development and reserve structurally distinct external ones.

3. Keep improving multipliers but target robust relative error and output demand,
   not only headline MAE. Multiplication supplies 56.15% of full semantics novel
   absolute error, with systematic overprediction: signed bias +90.3644 LUT4.
   Original graph reduces that bias to +71.0352. Test separate signed/high-half,
   sparse-support, sign-extension and output-truncation families with balanced
   complexity and measured ranges. Retain normalized and original syntax since
   their mapped costs can differ even for equivalent functions.

4. Expand the calibration domain and measure bound performance honestly. The
   full fit flags every novel test formula as outside its feature ranges.
   v5_original_ops is 2–6 in both fitting and calibration, but 7–9 for every novel
   formula; formula length exceeds the fitting maximum for 96/128 novel rows.
   Those are reliable domain alarms, not a calibrated risk estimator. Semantic
   descriptors collectively receive only ~0.61% of this ExtraTrees importance,
   and original_ops ~0.0011%, so input-range flags do not establish the causal
   mechanism of the regression. Develop OOD/residual diagnostics and calibration
   on new independent composition families; evaluate coverage, slack and cap
   errors together. A real abstention policy requires its own frozen experiment.

5. Increase independent family replication before stronger claims. The saved
   v5-next-design.txt recommends at least eight distinct connected family
   components per stratum, preferably twelve, with nested operand settings,
   plus separately reserved external application families. Preserve V5's exact
   scores and all failures as development evidence for that future experiment.

This audit verifies statistical computation and frozen consistency, not a
raw-netlist recount. It found a meaningful matched-baseline cost-prediction
