# One decision-aware feasible-benefit model — development protocol

Declared before any fits of this model. All six workloads are consumed development data. This experiment changes the learned decision target, not the measured-speedup labels or current predictor. No native measurement or LLM calls are authorized here.

Hypothesis: conditioning the normalized XGBoost target on hardware budget may reduce the wasted measurements demonstrated by SHA256's correctly ranked but area-infeasible highest-speedup candidates. Train expected *feasible* normalized cycle savings directly rather than asking the benefit predictor to rank unconstrained speedup. Failure to improve remains a result; no variants or hyperparameter/feature sweep follow.

One model uses the unchanged 31 hardware structural columns, plus one budget context: cap/(median training integrated LUT4 increment per tree operation × candidate tree operations). This scalar expresses area headroom relative to the already-used historical area heuristic. It uses training-only measured areas and public operation counts; no target hardware labels enter inputs. No ordered-descriptor bundle is reused. The single median scale is fit once on the original historical candidate rows, never cap replicas or target labels. Require a positive scale.

Fit three rows per successful measured candidate, for caps32/64/128 LUT4 and common12MHz. Utility is signed (baseline_cycles-candidate_cycles)/exact calibrated replacement count when device fit, routed minimum Fmax≥12 and integrated area increment≤cap; otherwise zero *decision utility*. An infeasible zero is not a measured slowdown, speedup label or imputed failure outcome. Failed/censored attempts are excluded from training and unknown utility scoring, remain charged when queried, and never become hardware-infeasibility labels. The measured-speedup predictor stays separate.

Each of20 bootstrap members samples n measured candidates with replacement. Every sampled candidate contributes all three cap contexts, weighted1/3 each; replicated cap rows are not independent labels and effective sample mass remains n. Use existing normalized XGB settings:80 trees, depth2, learning_rate.05, reg_lambda1, min_child_weight1, full row/column sampling, hist, n_jobs1, reg:squarederror. Model/tie seeds0..4; no tuning. Candidate-level bootstrap resampling and model-seed draw order match the existing normalized implementation. Constant complete utility targets use the same constant fallback. Intervals describe uncalibrated ensemble disagreement.

For target candidates and cap, predict mean signed feasible saved cycles/replacement and its ensemble spread. Rank by predicted feasible saved fraction: mean utility × public replacement count / public baseline cycles. This score is not a speedup or calibrated feasibility probability. Fixed zero-shot model only; provider releases outcomes solely on charged query, final acceptance checks actual paid correctness/device/area/timing and retains baseline. Same three-query allowance, shuffled tie order and independent final scoring as frozen controls. Search does not stop early based on uncalibrated utility.

Reuse exact archived history/public/excluded-family cohorts and runtime/source bindings for EDN, UD and SHA256. Check candidate/workload/family exclusions and postfilter groups. Other consumed workloads form historical training; these are not six fresh holdouts. Preserve original controls/predictions/results. Authenticate label-free data and freeze protocol/source hashes, model settings and target-label-free utility predictions before opening report labels.

Compare all10 frozen controls (frequency, merit, calibrated latency, historical cost heuristic, random, raw speedup XGB, pairwise ranker, normalized XGB, zero-shot GP, adaptive GP) at caps32/64/128, budgets1/2/3, seeds0..4. Primary: best verified feasible whole-program speedup, gap to best-verified reference and charged target measurement cost by budget. UD has a censored full pool; its gap is a lower bound on regret. Secondary: expected-utility MAE versus actual known utility, wasted known-infeasible queries, unknown queries and false beneficial utility predictions. Never report these as calibrated feasibility errors. Preserve all seeds/caps and negative outcomes. Historical available-label costs (including excluded/failed attempts), fitting counts, baseline/setup provenance, inference and target times remain separate; mixed historical timer scopes do not measure full end-to-end search time.
