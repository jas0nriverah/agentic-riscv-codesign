**The proposed lookahead needs a precise update contract.** Each actual paid refit recomputes input standardization and output mean/scale. Conditioning the current joint Gaussian holds these quantities fixed. After observing 00, fixed conditioning predicts candidate 14 at 59.789±21.862 LUT, while the archived refit gives 59.870±21.708. After 00 and 14, fixed conditioning predicts 11 at 60.422±21.861 versus the archived 60.719±21.642. These small but real differences disprove exact equivalence between the two procedures.

A future implementation must either reproduce the actual refit procedure inside hypothetical updates or explicitly introduce a fixed-preprocessing update policy. The latter is another declared change and needs its own one-step control. Refit-based rollout is an approximation with a changing empirical prior. Declare whether alpha is observation noise or numerical stabilization, use covariance in physical units with scale squared, and include cross-candidate covariance. No lookahead action or trajectory was evaluated here.

Treating calibrated benefit as known, for a cycle-reduction endpoint let `b_i = 1 − 1/speedup_i`, `v` be the best verified feasible cycle reduction, and `F_i` successful verification plus hardware feasibility. The one-query comparator is `(b_i − v)+ × P(F_i | paid data)`. Two-query lookahead averages the first verified improvement plus the best second-query expected improvement **after** observing the first outcome. The second maximization belongs inside the expectation. A fixed pair chosen before the first observation tests a different policy. The same benefit units and update model must be used for one-step and lookahead comparisons. Three-query receding-horizon search is not an exact three-step solution.

**Primary methods checked and their actual relevance.** These are adaptations to investigate, not novelty or reproduction claims.

- Gardner et al., *Bayesian Optimization with Inequality Constraints*, ICML 2014, §§3.1–3.2: observed feasible incumbent, zero improvement for an infeasible result, and retained constraint observations. Independent objective/constraint models give improvement times feasibility; dependent constraints need joint probability. This is the strongest simple decision baseline. [Published methods](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v32/gardner14.pdf).
- Zhang, Zhang and Frazier, *Constrained Two-step Look-Ahead Bayesian Optimization*, NeurIPS 2021, §§2.3, 3.2, 4 and 5.1: terminal feasible utility with correlated posterior updates; finite enumeration can replace continuous optimization. The linked PDF has a different title. Its experiments additionally score a separate recommendation with ≥0.975 probability for each constraint, so published curves are not the identical ISAForge paid-verification protocol. The method does not create covariance missing from our model. [Published PDF](https://papers.nips.cc/paper_files/paper/2021/file/685217557383cd194b4f10ae4b39eebf-Paper.pdf), [official metadata](https://papers.nips.cc/paper_files/paper/2021/hash/685217557383cd194b4f10ae4b39eebf-Abstract.html).
- Swersky, Snoek and Adams, *Multi-Task Bayesian Optimization*, NIPS 2013, §§2.2–3.3: input/task covariance learns transfer relationships and separately models evaluation cost. Target observations are needed to estimate task relationships; a new workload ID alone does not solve zero-shot transfer. A small shared-function/source-offset diagnostic would be our adaptation and cannot repair all within-source errors. [Published methods](https://papers.nips.cc/paper_files/paper/2013/file/f33ba15effa5c10e873bf3842afb46a6-Paper.pdf).
- Lee, Foygel Barber and Willett, *Distribution-free inference with hierarchical data*, arXiv:2306.06342v4, August 2, 2025, §2.2/Eq.6/Theorem1: group-aware coverage assumes hierarchical exchangeability and targets one new-group point. It does not guarantee simultaneous pool or adaptively selected-query coverage. Its infinity mass implies at least nine calibration groups for a potentially finite ordinary 90% quantile and nineteen for 95%; twelve total sources leave little room after fitting. [Versioned methods](https://arxiv.org/html/2306.06342v4).
- Tibshirani et al., *Conformal Prediction Under Covariate Shift*, NeurIPS 2019, §§2–2.1: weighted prediction requires invariant conditional labels, support overlap and a known density ratio for the stated guarantee. Source-dependent area offsets can violate that premise; reweighting candidate rows is not a calibration cure. [Published methods](https://papers.nips.cc/paper/2019/file/8fb21ee7a2207526da55a679f0332de2-Paper.pdf).
- Rezunov et al., CIDRE, ICCAD 2025 / arXiv:2509.15782v1, §III/Algorithm1 and §IV: established constrained enumeration/canonicalization, integrated synthesis, clock-aware greedy selection and covering lookahead. Its RV64IM ASIP Designer processor and multi-instruction evaluations differ from this PicoRV32 FPGA experiment. Its reported speedups are not comparable headline targets for our six/twelve-candidate single-instruction pools. [Primary methods](https://arxiv.org/html/2509.15782v1).

**Prioritized engineering sequence.** Keep the original architecture, inputs, gates and all fourteen controls. Do not implement these as a simultaneous model sweep.

1. **Make covariance/update behavior testable before planning.** Expose a read-only joint-posterior interface around the frozen model and specify one future update procedure. Verify its predicted paid update against the actual declared procedure, including scaler/output normalization and noise units. Report cross-candidate covariance and the movement of unmeasured area beliefs. This is the immediate blocker; the current analysis reconstructs the evidence but adds no runtime interface.
2. **Run one controlled decision experiment.** Compare one-step versus two-step terminal cycle-reduction utility with identical posterior, update procedure, cohort, ties and compute budget. Keep all fourteen controls. Add one prespecified simple diversity/final-incumbent fallback control so a claimed planning gain cannot merely be a trivial avoidance of three similar risky queries. Seal settings before development scoring; preserve losses. Do not claim that lookahead alone repairs the present covariance.
3. **If discrimination remains the blocker, test one hardware-model correction.** A cost-heuristic prior mean plus a learned residual is a feasible, tightly scoped hypothesis: outside support it preserves a structural cost prior instead of reverting to one global area mean. It must beat that heuristic under the same budget and retain prior wins; lower area MAE alone is insufficient. A representation/distance change is an alternative isolated hypothesis, not another concurrent version. Avoid blindly pooling more history, source-offset-only fixes, feature/kernel sweeps and another speedup learner.
4. **Qualify an untouched source cohort using the working adapter.** `g723_enc` is a static scout, not a supported workload yet. Original 256 samples/checksum24284, code/memory fit, helpers, verifier, twelve-formula pool and same-candidate 0.5%/ten-execution opportunity must qualify. Preserve every rejection and low feasible-headroom outcome. Freeze models/cohorts/controller and the full source sequence before candidate labels; collect live queries before oracle-only outcomes. Two or three new accepted sources would be a useful next cohort, not a significance guarantee.
5. **Complete timing and joint-set evidence later.** Timing is currently checked but never restrictive. A future baseline-compatible clock contract can test genuine cycles-versus-clock tradeoffs, with existing 12 MHz primary results unchanged. Then reuse reviewed compatible teammate slot/overlap components for jointly executed and synthesized sets of one to three instructions. Never add individual speedups or area increments. Keep live LLM, cloud, demo and RTL rewriting outside this immediate experiment.

They illustrate why clock policy must be explicit before future labels.

**Acceptance and costs.** Primary comparisons remain best verified feasible cycle reduction and measured-pool regret at queries one/two/three, +64 LUT4, 12 MHz. Require improvement over the strongest prespecified heuristic, not only an older GP. Also retain +32/+128, exact source/family exclusions, Brier/interval diagnostics by source, false-feasible decisions, native count/time and host computation. Distinguish ranking, feasibility calibration, interval coverage and selection utility. No-op references have undefined gain fractions; unknown infrastructure outcomes are not negative labels.

Keep historical labels/failures and excluded-but-collected attempts visible, while separating measurement data required by each policy. Simple untrained heuristics should not be charged ML fitting as a fictional prerequisite. Training, qualification/baseline, target search, oracle reference and refits/inference are separate scopes; overlapping timers are not summed. Reaching an oracle early in a fixed three-query run does not save attempts or establish stopping. The earlier UTC/monotonic disagreements remain preserved.
