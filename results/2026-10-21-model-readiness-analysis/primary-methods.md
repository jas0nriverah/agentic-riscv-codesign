# Focused primary-method review

Reviewed October 21, 2026 for the model/selection-readiness analysis. These papers justify bounded adaptations, not novelty or ISAForge performance claims. No model fits, LLM calls or native evaluations were performed. The earlier research-foundation review and its unresolved Sun/Hussein access gaps remain preserved.

| Method / exact source | Method sections reviewed | Application and limit |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Gardner et al., *Bayesian Optimization with Inequality Constraints*, ICML 2014 ([paper](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v32/gardner14.pdf)) | §§3.1–3.2, PDF pp.3–4 | Feasible-incumbent improvement weighted by constraint probability; factorization assumes independence. Our analytic objective with learned hardware constraints is an adaptation, not full constrained EI. Improve the demonstrated representation before replacing the algorithm. |
| Snoek et al., *Practical Bayesian Optimization of Machine Learning Algorithms*, NeurIPS 2012 ([v2](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1206.2944v2)) | §3.2, PDF pp.5–6 | Model log duration and weight by expected inverse duration under independence. This rewards duration uncertainty and differs from dividing by expected duration. It motivates consistent timer labels/runtime context and a no-cost-weight control, not a new framework. |
| Chen et al., *Learning to Optimize Tensor Programs*, NeurIPS 2018, AutoTVM ([v4](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1805.08166v4)) | §4, Figure 3, Eq.(4), PDF pp.4–5 | Contextual program structure supports transfer better than workload-specific coordinates. Adapt the structural principle with a small ordered-operator/immediate bundle. Tensor-program results do not establish integrated-processor area accuracy or justify a neural tree model on 33 labels. |
| Romano et al., *Conformalized Quantile Regression*, NeurIPS 2019 ([paper](https://papers.nips.cc/paper/8613-conformalized-quantile-regression.pdf)) | §§3–4, Eqs.(6)–(8), Algorithm 1, Theorem 1 | Quantile models need untouched calibration scores and exchangeability for marginal coverage. Row-wise calibration would ignore workload dependence. Quantile regression alone does not repair the current intervals or establish calibration under adaptive candidate sampling. |
| Lee, Barber and Willett, *Distribution-free inference with hierarchical data* ([v1](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2306.06342v1)) | §§1.1,2.2, Eq.(6), Theorem 1, PDF pp.6–7 | HCP gives equal calibration-group weight and reserves mass at infinity. The report derives the finite-group limit for this specific split construction; it is not a prohibition on every Bayesian interval. Curated candidate pools also need an appropriate sampling argument. |
| Yao et al., *ReAct*, ICLR 2023 ([v3](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2210.03629v3)) | §2 and §§3.2–3.3, PDF pp.3–6 | A bounded action/observation loop is a useful agent design precedent. Its action-only control motivates testing whether the language layer adds value. QA and interactive-environment results do not establish hardware-selection gains. Use concise decision summaries rather than requesting hidden reasoning traces. |
| Liu et al., *Large Language Models to Enhance Bayesian Optimization*, ICLR 2024, LLAMBO ([official paper](https://proceedings.iclr.cc/paper_files/paper/2024/file/84b8d9fcb4e262fcd429544697e1e720-Paper-Conference.pdf)) | §§2.2,4,6–8, PDF pp.4–9 | Initialization, surrogate and proposal components can be used separately. The study concerns hyperparameter tuning and acknowledges increased inference compute. It supports testing a small language decision layer, not replacing our numeric predictor or importing its performance claims. |

The proposed agent keeps deterministic extraction, numeric prediction, paid measurement and final acceptance. It receives four narrow tools and the same information/budget as non-agent comparators. Start with consumed-data interface validation, then freeze model, prompts, limits and repeated-run protocol before prospective testing. Five model trajectories are repetitions of one workload, not five independent tasks. Shared historical measurement cost, failed attempts and inference overhead remain charged and visible.

One compact hardware-descriptor ablation is justified by observed area-boundary failures. A duration-context study is separate and first needs matching timer definitions. Additional cycle models, GNNs, RL enumeration, multi-agent orchestration, area-only version searches and candidate-wise confidence guarantees are not supported by the present evidence.
