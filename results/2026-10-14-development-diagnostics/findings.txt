V6 fitting-only seed and concentration diagnostic

Scope: 1,665 frozen V5 fitting rows, the same five frozen component folds,
ExtraTrees with 300 trees/minimum leaf 1/max_features 1.0, and seeds
2026101421–2026101425. Three views × five seeds × five folds = 75 fits.
No calibration or test rows enter any fit, metric or seed-based comparison.
This study was designed after V5 outcomes were inspected and remains development
evidence. It does not revise V5 or produce a new independent test result.

Mean macro-stratum fitting OOF MAE across seeds (LUT4), sample standard deviation:
  original_graph, 74 features:    5.420463 ± 0.053502; range [5.362089, 5.474512]
  dual_graph, 194 features:      5.305404 ± 0.039051; range [5.263464, 5.348280]
  dual_semantics, 313 features:  5.246948 ± 0.034276; range [5.197610, 5.282846]

Dual graph beats original graph in all five paired seeds by a mean 0.115058
LUT4 (2.12%). Full semantics beats original graph in all five seeds by 0.173514
(3.20%) and beats dual graph in all five seeds by only 0.058456 (1.10%). These
observed differences are stable in direction across the five seeds; they are
not explained by an isolated lucky seed. Five seeds do not establish a
population confidence interval, and OOF selection remains development evidence.

The small semantic gain is concentrated in multiplication. Relative to dual
graph, its multiplier MAE improves by 0.919370, contributing -0.102152 to the
nine-stratum macro difference. The other eight strata together contribute
+0.043696, opposing that gain. Full semantics increases arithmetic MAE by
0.132509, mask-logic MAE by 0.186084 and variable-shift MAE by 0.406900 versus
dual graph. Its mean multiplier accuracy improvement therefore does not imply
broadly improved composition prediction.

Arithmetic MAE is lower for dual graph than original graph in all five seeds.
The multiplier comparison between those two changes direction with the seed.
Multiplier worst underprediction across seeds remains substantial:
173.71–196.39 LUT4 for original graph, 177.31–203.33 for dual graph, and
175.66–210.57 for full semantics. Small average signed bias masks large
individual residuals; it is not a calibrated safety margin.

The error distribution remains concentrated in every view/seed. The worst 17
of 1,665 fitting OOF rows (~1%) produce 25.1–27.0% of pooled absolute error;
the worst 84 (~5%) produce 54.8–57.1%. Multiplication accounts for approximately
39.1–41.4% of pooled error. All cases remain in the reported metrics.

Repeated seeds cannot repair structural failures. For example, full semantics
predicts add(in0,mulhu(in1,in1)) at a five-seed mean 2603.49 against the recorded
2253 target, while the between-seed prediction standard deviation is only 12.84.
For srli(mulhu(addi(in0,-1023),xori(in1,3)),16), the mean is 2783.90 against
2926, with standard deviation 1.80. Stable predictions can remain substantially
wrong. The saved worst-development-cases.json retains ten such diagnostics.

Practical direction for the next lineage

Dual graph is a reasonable simpler candidate: its 194-feature view retains a
small but consistent fitting improvement over the 74-feature original graph
and has the strongest arithmetic OOF result among the three. Keep original
graph as the essential simpler control. Keep semantic features as an explicit
multiplier-focused ablation; this study does not justify treating their narrow
MAE gain as a robust improvement across families. Their removal or revised
estimator still needs separately reserved families and an external test.

Prioritize more independent, deeper arithmetic/shared/shift families and
multiplier demand/reuse regimes before another hyperparameter expansion.
Evaluate worst underprediction and family calibration as well as macro MAE.
Seed ensembles slightly reduce OOF MAE but do not address domain coverage or
turn seed dispersion into a confidence interval. Use the previous V5 failure
analysis to design a new study rather than replacing its reported result.

Fewer selected columns do not automatically reduce inference work: the frozen
V5 extractor computes every channel and semantic feature for every view.
Actual extraction savings require a separate view-specific extractor change
and an explicit cold/warm runtime benchmark in the new lineage.
